What is CV?

CV (Curriculum Vitae) is a detailed professional document showing education, projects, research, experience, publications, certifications and academic achievements.

CV Structure

  • Personal Information
  • Professional Summary
  • Education Details
  • Projects & Research
  • Experience
  • Publications
  • Skills & Certifications
  • References

Academic CV

Academic CV focuses on research work, publications, conferences, workshops, teaching experience and academic awards.

Professional CV Tips

  • Use formal formatting
  • Highlight achievements clearly
  • Add publications properly
  • Keep sections organized
  • Maintain accuracy

Common CV Mistakes

  • Unorganized sections
  • Too much unnecessary data
  • Formatting inconsistency
  • Missing references
  • Grammar mistakes

Why CV Matters?

CV is essential for academic admissions, higher studies, research jobs and professional career opportunities. A strong CV creates credibility.
