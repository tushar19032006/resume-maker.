What is Resume?

Resume is a professional document that highlights your education, projects, skills, certifications and work experience to apply for jobs.

Types of Resume

  • Chronological Resume – Best for experienced candidates
  • Functional Resume – Focus on skills
  • Combination Resume – Mix of skills + experience
  • Fresher Resume – For students & graduates

Resume Rules

  • Keep 1–2 pages
  • Professional font
  • Clean layout
  • Use bullet points
  • Proofread properly

Common Mistakes

  • Spelling mistakes
  • Too many colors
  • Fake details
  • Long paragraphs
  • Irrelevant info

How to Make Impressive Resume

  • Write strong summary
  • Add measurable achievements
  • Highlight technical skills
  • Customize for job role
  • Use professional design

Why Resume Matters?

Resume creates first impression before interview. A strong professional resume increases recruiter response and improves job opportunities.
