What is Resume?

Resume is a professional document that highlights your education, projects, skills, certifications and work experience to apply for jobs.
A resume is a simple document that tells employers about your education, skills, experience, and achievements. It helps companies understand who you are and why you are suitable for a job.

Types of Resume

  • Chronological Resume – This type of resume shows your education and work experience from latest to oldest. It is commonly used by experienced professionals.
  • Functional Resume – A functional resume focuses more on skills and abilities instead of job experience. It is useful for freshers or people changing careers.
  • Combination Resume – This resume is a mix of skills and work experience. It gives equal importance to both.
  • Fresher Resume – A fresher resume is not a type of resume,but it is made for students or beginners who have little or no work experience. It mainly highlights education, skills, and projects.

Resume Rules

  • Keep the resume clean and easy to read
  • Use simple and professional formatting
  • Add only important and relevant details
  • Check spelling and grammar carefully
  • Organize information properly with headings
  • Keep the content short and clear
  • Keep 1–2 pages
  • Professional font
  • Clean layout
  • Use bullet points
  • Proofread properly

Common Resume Mistakes

  • Making spelling or grammar mistakes
  • Using poor formatting or messy design
  • Adding false or incorrect details
  • Making the resume too long
  • Writing too much unnecessary information
  • Using unprofessional email IDs
  • Not customizing the resume for the job role

How to Make Impressive Resume

  • Write a clear career objective
  • Use simple and confident language
  • Highlight your important skills
  • Update the resume according to the job role
  • Customize for job role
  • Add measurable achievements
  • Use a neat and professional design
  • Add projects, certifications, and experience

Why Resume Matters?

A resume is important because it creates the first impression on employers. It helps companies quickly understand your skills, qualifications, and abilities. A good resume can increase your chances of getting selected for interviews and job opportunities.
